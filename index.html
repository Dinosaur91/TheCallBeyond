Before You Answer the Call

Please read this carefully before proceeding.

The Path

Choose your House, find the password entrusted to it, and follow the path that opens before you.

The path is not complicated and should take approximately 5–10 minutes to complete.

Complete your path... but for the ones who need more, the world is your oyster.

The Password

If you cannot find the password, you may be trying to find it among the living.

Claiming the Reward

Reaching the rewards page does not automatically claim its points.

To make a valid claim, capture a screenshot of the rewards page and post it in the House Cup – Westpay WhatsApp group.

Where order matters, the first valid screenshot posted in the group will claim the available reward.

Your screenshot must clearly show the revealed House symbol or symbols and the points displayed on the page.

Remember Your Journey

Complete your journey using the same device and browser.

Your progress may not follow you into another browser, another device or private-browsing mode.

Do not clear your browser data while the challenge is active. What the browser forgets, the gods will not restore.
